# CGLab

**Institute of Molecular Biology and Biotechnology (IMBB-FORTH), Heraklion, Crete**

We study the evolution of genomes, proteins, and gene families across the tree of life using computational and comparative approaches. Our work spans olfactory receptor evolution, protein structure prediction, tRNA biology, and the molecular basis of biological complexity.

🌐 **Website:** [cgenomicslab.github.io](https://cgenomicslab.github.io)

## How to cite our software

Each repository with published software includes a `CITATION.cff` file. Click the "Cite this repository" button in the repo sidebar to get the citation in APA or BibTeX format.
